# Gagron: mapped fort and river context

The old scene was a generated triangle, with invented palace blocks, settlement and a generalized three-sided water shape. It also displayed a c. 1450 version without evidence for its detailed form. These have been withdrawn.

The current research scene uses [OSM fort area 254041048](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/254041048) as an area cue, and separately maps wall segments [139084826](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/139084826), [254041049](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/254041049), [254041050](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/254041050), [254041051](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/254041051), [254041052](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/254041052), [254041055](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/254041055) and [254041056](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/254041056). The area measures about 10.1 ha in local projection; UNESCO’s component property is about 23 ha, so these are not interchangeable. The mapped area is neither a surveyed property nor the wall circuit. Wall segments are incomplete and their render height and thickness are illustrative. © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL.

The river context follows the mapped modern water-area polygons for [Ahu](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/139084736), [Kali Sindh](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/139084753) and [another adjacent river-water polygon](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/260795619), cropped to the local scene. Polygons cannot establish historic or seasonal shorelines, and the water level and banks are illustrative. [UNESCO/ICOMOS](https://whc.unesco.org/document/153037) describes the fort at the confluence and the northeast access through two gates; these gates and their path are not located by this pass.

The terrain uses 169 SRTM30 samples obtained from [OpenTopodata](https://www.opentopodata.org/datasets/srtm/) on 1 November 2026, spaced 180 m apart about the UNESCO component reference 24.626389°N, 76.187222°E. The 281–403 m sample range and interpolated terrain are regional context, not accurate fort slopes or riverbank elevations. Buildings, settlement, roads, gates, underwater topography and dated phases remain unmodeled or unverified. A registered architectural survey, detailed terrain, current aerials and phase-specific evidence are needed before a faithful reconstruction can be claimed.
