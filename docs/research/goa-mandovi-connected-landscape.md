# Goa: Mandovi estuary landscape and Reis Magos

This is a research and implementation backlog, not a claim that a continuous or historically accurate Goa has been built. The existing local Reis Magos scene covers roughly 360 × 450 m; its ground is an illustrative linear slope, not surveyed terrain. The church is a generic mass on a mapped footprint, the cemetery has only a mapped boundary, and the stair linking fort levels has an inferred route. Its fort walls and gate are not measured. See [the existing ground-truth pass](reis-magos-ground-truth-pass.md) for the sources and remaining caveats. The present-day outlines cannot establish appearance in any earlier campaign year.

## The connected setting

[Goa Tourism's Aguada account](https://goa-tourism.com/fort/fort-aguada/) describes the Mandovi entrance as the approach to Velha Goa and names Reis Magos, Cabo and Gaspar Dias as elements of its defence; Aguada was completed later, in 1612. It also says Gaspar Dias no longer exists. The [Government of Goa's Reis Magos account](https://www.goa.gov.in/places/reis-magos-fort/) places the fort on the Bardez bank, northeast of Aguada, with a spring to its east and the church at its base. Its [church account](https://www.goa.gov.in/places/the-reis-magos-church/) describes the hamlet and church steps. The [National Institute of Oceanography publication](https://drs.nio.res.in/drs/bitstream/handle/2264/4727/Marit_Archaeol_India_2015_207a.pdf) also discusses the historic forts of the Mandovi and Goa coast.

These accounts support a regional relationship, not exact cannon ranges, positions, sightlines, paths, ferry terminals, bank geometry, historical vegetation or a present-day survey. For the game, distinguish the river mouth and opposing banks, the inland waterway to Old Goa, the local built settlement, and connections north to Chapora and south toward Mormugao and Cabo de Rama. These are candidate research areas, not complete game geography. Each fort and settlement needs date-specific evidence before being shown in a past campaign.

The existing authored Aguada–Reis Magos link incorrectly called the connection a ferry, even though the two sites lie on the same side of the Mandovi. It is now a provisional overland gameplay link; its actual path, duration and historical use remain unverified. The separately authored Reis Magos–Old Goa ferry link is also an unverified gameplay abstraction, not evidence for a particular crossing or service.

## Work needed before a faithful playable landscape

1. Obtain an appropriately licensed terrain model and water/coastline data covering both banks and the full Mandovi entrance; document horizontal and vertical datums, resolution, capture dates, accuracy and gaps. A coarse elevation grid may support the regional landscape but cannot resolve fort steps, walls or retaining terraces.
2. Georeference current aerial photographs and available restoration drawings; compare their dates and control points against the fort outline, shoreline, roads, village, church and cemetery. Map Aguada, Cabo and the approximate historic area of Gaspar Dias independently; do not invent its lost footprint.
3. Acquire restoration plans, detailed photographs and, where possible, measured site survey or photogrammetry for Reis Magos. Register the real entrance sequence, changes in elevation, stairs and ramps, walls and batteries, and documented cannon positions. Record every feature as measured, documented, inferred or unknown, with source and date. Existing conjectural pavilions and staircase must not be treated as evidence.
4. Build a continuous regional terrain with local high-detail areas, connected by roads and river travel only where supported or clearly marked as gameplay proposals. Record historic land use, settlement, churches, farms, port facilities, crossings, tidal water and monsoon constraints from dated evidence. Dated terrain and buildings must be separate from the modern survey baseline.
5. Verify walkability, transitions, scale, viewpoints, water access and interactions against references and a human site/heritage review. Test campaign movement and persistence separately. An automatically connected navigation graph does not prove a faithful route or a playable campaign.

The scene and campaign do not yet provide this continuous world, surveyed levels, verified guns, complete surroundings, or authentic past phases. The next useful acquisition is the Reis Magos restoration survey and an appropriately licensed high-resolution Goa terrain/aerial dataset.
