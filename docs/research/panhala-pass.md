# Panhala Fort: research pass

Batch-0 run, 2026-11-15 (scheduled run). Site id `panhala`. Tier reached **T1** (status `needs-plan`). Curation: `tools/trace/curation/panhala.mjs`; trace: `src/levels/panhala.trace.js`; level: `src/levels/panhala-t1.js`. Standing rules: no invented heights, no invented gates, no constructed layer. The level is registered in `src/levels/alpha-x0.js`.

## Mapped (OpenStreetMap and terrain)
Outline is OSM way 120880303 (36 vertices, Q7131008); a second way, Pavangad (120880304), lies beside it and was not drawn. No wall line is mapped. Drawn: 5 markers (Teen Darwaza, Sajja Kothi, North Gate and two ruins), 31 paths, 6 buildings, 1 water body.

Terrain is Terrarium z15 (about 30 m SRTM-derived, smoothed), so scarps, ditches and terraces are not resolved. Only one era, "today", is drawn: no earlier era was carried back because no dated source was read.

## Plans and images found
Commons search returned Bombay Presidency gazetteers and gate photos; no plan.

## Not done in this run
Written history (builders, sieges, dates) was not researched or cited: the sandbox web fetch was refused for unattended runs, and search returned links only. None of it is in the scene. No image was downloaded or used as a texture.

## Missing evidence for T2
