# Gorkhatri, Peshawar: fort-fidelity pass (alpha-W batch)

Date of pass: 2026-11-15. Branch `fort-fidelity`. Trace: `data/traces/gorkhatri-q5586577.trace.json` (auto-T1, `tools/build-t1.mjs`; scene via `src/levels/t1.js`).

**Method and limit.** Text sources only, read through WebSearch/WebFetch. The sandbox cannot view plan images or photographs, and most pages reached
us as tool summaries. Wikipedia and its mirrors are secondary. **No plan, survey drawing or dated photograph was registered to the scene**, so
nothing here reaches T2. Every non-OSM claim below is text evidence, not geometry.

Not a classic fort: the site is a caravanserai and archaeological complex. It sits in the batch for letter coverage.

## Mapped (OpenStreetMap, ODbL; Terrarium DEM)

Outline way 841242375 ("Gorkhatri Excavations", `historic=ruins`) is only about 31 x 38 m (about 0.10 ha), far smaller than the documented 160 x 160 m compound, so it is the excavated core, **not the enclosure**. Also markers 2551981154 and 10608110812, walls 841251799/800/801, 19 buildings, 152 roads.

## Inferred / drawn as inferred

The compound edge is not drawn. The outline must not be read as the caravanserai boundary.
