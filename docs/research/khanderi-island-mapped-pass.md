# Khanderi: island, fort area and lighthouse

The previous playable site used an invented oval and repeating bastions, with a catalogue position roughly half a kilometre west of Khanderi. The present-day scene now uses an approximate island-centre anchor, 18.7043°N, 72.8133°E. This is a map projection origin, not a surveyed control point.

The island shape follows [OpenStreetMap coastline way 22825778](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/22825778); the separate [fort area](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/281255137), [landing polygon](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/622254485), [landing footway](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/622254486), and [lighthouse point](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/926466578) are present-day mapped features. © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL. The fort area is not a wall circuit, UNESCO property boundary or measured site plan. The old generic walls and invented buildings have been removed rather than asserted as real.

The [DGLL master ledger](https://www.dgll.nic.in/sites/default/files/2022-12/mm14.pdf) reports a 17 m octagonal tower at 18°42′11.43″N, 72°48′47.35″E; the OSM point is about 33 m away. A [Mumbai Port document](https://mumbaiport.gov.in/WriteReadData/RTF1984/1695205402.pdf) instead gives 30 m and a rounded coordinate much farther south. The model uses the OSM point and 17 m height as a provisional cue, with estimated footprint; the discrepancies must be reconciled with a measured tower and on-site control. The DGLL webpage describes the present lighthouse as lit in 1867, while its ledger gives a different commissioning entry. Do not treat either height, position or date as resolved by this model.

Terrain uses 40 m-spaced [SRTM30](https://www.opentopodata.org/datasets/srtm/) samples obtained 1 November 2026; 52 samples with centres inside the mapped island are retained, with interpolation and an illustrative minimum ground height. Coastal pixels and 30 m source resolution cannot resolve ramparts, terrain, paths or the jetty; water depth is not modeled. The shoreline, wall, buildings and landing need a current orthophoto and measured survey. The former 1679 scene was removed because this present-day mapping does not establish its historical plan. Underi, reefs, Thal, tide and the regional harbour are outside the local scene.
