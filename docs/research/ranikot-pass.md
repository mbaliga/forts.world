# Ranikot: fort-fidelity pass (alpha-W batch)

Date of pass: 2026-11-15. Branch `fort-fidelity`. Trace: `data/traces/ranikot.trace.json` (auto-T1, `tools/build-t1.mjs`; scene via `src/levels/t1.js`).

**Method and limit.** Text sources only, read through WebSearch/WebFetch. The sandbox cannot view plan images or photographs, and most pages reached
us as tool summaries. Wikipedia and its mirrors are secondary. **No plan, survey drawing or dated photograph was registered to the scene**, so
nothing here reaches T2. Every non-OSM claim below is text evidence, not geometry.

## Documented (text sources)

| Fact | Value | Source | Strength |
|---|---|---|---|
| Origin unsettled: Scythian (70 BC-46 AD), Arab, or Talpur (19th century) are all claimed | stated; Abro dates it pre-Islamic and finds one Gupta-period inscription and iron arrowheads | The Friday Times, "Great Wall of Sindh" (2019) | secondary, contested |
| Talpur annals claim an early 19th-century build costing 1.2 million rupees; Salman Rashid rejects this but sees extensive Talpur-era repairs | stated | Dawn, 15 April 2018 | secondary (author's field visits 1980, 1999, 2017-18) |
| Coins found inside: three in the style of Sultan Muzaffar (1401-11), one attributed to Abu Said Mirza (mid-15th c.) | stated | Dawn | secondary |
| Gates: Sann (east), Mohan (west), Shahper (south); the Friday Times adds Amri (north-east) | stated; Dawn calls Shahper a decay gap, not a true gate | both | secondary, conflict on gate count |
| Inner forts: Miri in the middle; Shergarh (Shahgarh) on a hilltop above it, attributed to the Talpurs | stated | both | secondary; Friday Times also names a Mohan fortress without position |
| The Ranni (Mohan Nai) stream enters at Mohan Gate and leaves at Sann Gate | stated | Dawn | secondary |
| Late-1990s restoration at the Sann Gate bridge piers added crenulations and stairs; Shahper wall rebuilt 2017 with lime mortar and enlarged for truck access | stated | Dawn | secondary |

**No source reached gives the wall length, height or thickness.** The "26 km" figure that circulates was not found in any page reached and is not used.

## Mapped (OpenStreetMap, ODbL; Terrarium DEM)

Sann Gate node 1454871397; wall ways 416605212 and 416605213 "Ranikot Fort Wall" (`height=5`, `material=brick`, `wall=castle_wall`: a community tag, not independently sourced); Mohan Nai stream way 416606036. The 800 m window shows only a sliver of a wall that runs for kilometres; the mapped wall fragments are not a closed circuit.

## Inferred / drawn as inferred

No circuit, no gates other than the Sann marker, no inner fort. The OSM 5 m wall height is carried as mapped, flagged as unverified.

## Missing for T2

The wall circuit (a survey or satellite trace), wall height and thickness, positions of Mohan, Shahper and Amri gates, Miri and Shergarh footprints, a settled date. The build window had to be widened because the 30 m DEM cannot resolve the gap at Sann Gate: with the default 400 m window the gate marker was unreachable in the smoke test.
