# Goa regional terrain prototype

The standalone `goa-terrain.html` is a present-day, regional-scale research view. It is not a continuous walking-scale game world, a completed Goa landscape or a historical reconstruction. Its rectangular extent (73.64–74.36° E, 14.87–15.82° N) includes neighbouring areas as well as Goa; it is not clipped to the state boundary.

The 400 × 528 grid uses 12 Mapzen/Tilezen Terrarium zoom-10 elevation tiles obtained 1 November 2026 from the [AWS terrain tiles dataset](https://registry.opendata.aws/terrain-tiles/). Tile zoom and grid spacing do not establish native source resolution or survey accuracy. Elevations are vertically exaggerated six times for visibility. Coastal cells can sample water or land incorrectly; a check near Reis Magos returned -32 m, which is not a credible fort elevation. Negative ground values are clamped in the view, which is a display correction, not a surveyed height.

The colours come from [ESA WorldCover 2021 v200](https://esa-worldcover.org/en/data-access), tiles N12E072 and N15E072. The source classifies land cover at 10 m, but the viewer samples four points per display cell in each axis and shows approximately 200 m cells. A sampled built-up pixel clears the cell of tree symbols; this misses small or obscured buildings, buildings erected since 2021, and unsampled built-up pixels. It cannot guarantee that all present homes are empty. Water is also generalized, and in a check near the Mandovi an urban class obscured water; narrow rivers, shorelines and estuaries require a proper vector hydrography layer. Trees are symbolic clusters in sampled tree-cover cells, not actual individual trees, species or historical vegetation. © ESA WorldCover project 2021 / Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data (2021), distributed under CC BY 4.0.

Four markers come from the existing catalogue (Aguada, Cabo de Rama, Reis Magos and Chapora); five provisional additions are Terekhol, Corjuem, Alorna, Rachol and Colvale. Government sources identify these sites; the Rachol and Colvale points follow [OpenStreetMap way 1425373389](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/1425373389) and [way 1222668698](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/1222668698), © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL. The mapped Rachol site and surviving gate have different coordinates. Other coordinates are catalogue estimates or public listings, not surveyed site control. Some additional forts, including Mormugao, are known but not yet placed: the public OpenStreetMap query timed out, and the inventory is incomplete. [Goa Government](https://www.goa.gov.in/what_to_see/forts/) and [Goa University research](https://irgu.unigoa.ac.in/drs/bitstream/handle/unigoa/3902/gopalrao_v_2003.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1) document additional sites and are leads, not evidence that all have been located.

To progress: acquire and independently validate detailed coastline and river polygons, state boundary, higher-resolution terrain and a current building footprint dataset; mask trees against buildings at a resolution that can actually resolve them; reconcile a complete fort inventory with individual location evidence; and integrate the result with local scenes and ground-level travel. Verify spatial registration and visual comparison with reference imagery before calling it accurate.
