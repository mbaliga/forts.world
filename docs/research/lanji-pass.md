# Old Fort and temples, Lanji (Balaghat, Madhya Pradesh) - research pass 1

Site id `old-fort-and-temples-lanji-q116878170`, batch 157, 21.500361 N 80.544056 E. Status: research pass 1 only; below T1.

## Documented (secondary sources, not drawings)

- [ASI Jabalpur circle, Old Fort Lanji](https://asijabalpur.gov.in/monuments-detail/old-fort-9/index.html): ruined fort surrounded by a moat, with a brick fortification wall and bastions. No builder named. Lanji's rulers were feudatories of the Kalachuris of Ratanpur per a Bilaspur inscription of about 1114 CE; a Lanji inscription names a Yadava chief, Ramanayaka. Rajputs first, later Gonds. Notifications listed: CP No. 110 of 24/11/1906 and CP No. C-72 of 11/4/1925.
- Leads not read: Koteshwar Mahadev temple at Lanji is described by several sites as combining military and temple architecture; not verified here.

## Conflicts

1. The page gives no builder and no date for the standing brick fort; the 1114 CE inscription dates the rulers, not the walls.
2. The notification entries do not say "protected monument" in so many words.
