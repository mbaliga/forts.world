| Seven gates | stated; none named | same | secondary |

Hill height, wall circuits, bastions and dimensions are not stated.

## Mapped (OpenStreetMap, ODbL; Terrarium DEM)

Outline way 1024333331, tagged only `historic=fort` with no name, about 2.0 ha (214 x 196 m). It lies **540 m from the Wikidata point**; the two Smith & Newton records for the same fort (r285, r286) fall about 300-500 m north of the Wikidata point, between the two. The match to the "Bastion and Gateway" monument is **unverified**: the outline may be the hill fort only, and the bastion and gateway may stand in the town.  Two `natural=bare_rock` ways nearby.
