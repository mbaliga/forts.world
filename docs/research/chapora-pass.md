## Mapped (OpenStreetMap, ODbL)

Perimeter: outer ring of relation 15959326 (about 296 x 198 m, 3.66 ha; tagged `historic=castle`, `ruins=fort`). Footways inside it (ways 198262198, 198262207,
945601551 and others). Outside it, and **not part of the fort**: jetty road, village buildings and walls (ways 317904373, 317904386, 1358279666),
retaining walls (431878279, 1358279667), crematorium, tanks, the present coast (431878274, 1224812281, 1489929960).
No OSM element carries a height.

## Inferred (flagged `inferred` in the trace)

- **Historic eras.** The present perimeter, coast and sea are carried back to 1617 and 1717. The 1617 extent in particular may differ.
- **Heights.** None. Everything is a kerb-high plan line.

## Not drawn, although text describes it

Wall faces, the main gate and the river and sea gates labelled on Lopes fig. 25, bastions (the perimeter's projections are not identified), the tunnels, tombstones,
chapel, the 1717 additions. None has a plan position in a source I could read; the earlier scene's guessed gates and tunnels stay removed.

## Conflicts

- Builder: Portuguese in 1617 (Goa Government) versus Adil Shah's Shahpoora, completed 1617 (Wikipedia).
- Sambhaji: 1684 (Goa Government) versus 1683 (Wikipedia, truncated).
- Bastions/tunnel: attributed to 1717 by Wikipedia only.
