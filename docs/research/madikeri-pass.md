# Madikeri Fort: research pass

Alpha-U batch, 2026-11-14. Site id `madikeri-fort-q6727744`. Tier reached **T1** (status `needs-plan`). Curation: `tools/trace/curation/madikeri-fort-q6727744.mjs`; trace: `src/levels/madikeri-fort-q6727744.trace.js`. No plan, survey drawing or dated image was found, so nothing here is traced from a plan and no constructed layer is drawn. Standing rules: no invented heights, no invented gates, present-day roads and buildings on the "today" view.

## Mapped (OpenStreetMap and terrain)
- OSM relation 6432140 (historic=fort, Wikidata Q6727744) is the outline: 202 x 259 m, 34,323 m2 (3.4 ha, 8.5 acres), five-cornered. Two wall lines (ways 92384881 and 92384893, barrier=city_wall) are mapped; the Omkareshwara Temple compound wall (way 92384943) is a temple wall, not a fort wall.
- Thirteen buildings (the Madikeri Museum, tagged "museum in an old church", the Omkareshwara Temple, a restaurant, a shopping complex), a stream, a memorial marker and 48 town roads are mapped. The terrain grid reads 1,170 m at the outline centroid.

## Documented in text (Wikipedia, not placed unless stated)
- Wikipedia: built in the second half of the 17th century by Mudduraja; Tipu Sultan rebuilt it in granite and renamed it Jaffarabad (uncited); Dodda Vira Rajendra took the fort in 1790; Linga Rajendra II renovated the palace in 1812-14 (cited to the Karnataka State Gazetteer, 1965); British additions in 1834 (uncited); St Mark's Church of 1859 (cited to Penny, 1922), now the museum.
- Wikipedia: two stone elephant statues at the north-east entry; the palace is now the Deputy Commissioner's office. No wall, gate or size figure is given.

## Inferred
- The 1812 era is the mapped outline and two wall lines carried back. The temple (not read here) and the 1859 church are left out of it.

## Conflicts and doubts
- The ruler is "Linga Rajendra II" on the page; older notes say "Lingarajendra Wodeyar" and the page does not say they are the same.
- The 1971 takeover of the church is cited to a 1927 source that cannot support it. The page does not mention the Omkareshwara Temple.

## Missing evidence for T2
- A survey or plan from the Karnataka archaeology department: gate positions, the palace footprint (not identified among the mapped buildings), the elephant statues, wall heights.
