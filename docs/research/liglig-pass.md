# Liglig / Ligligkot (Gorkha, Nepal) - research pass 1

Site id `liglig-q6546674`, batch 739, 28.06223 N 84.526696 E (Wikipedia: 28 03 44 N 84 31 34 E, 1,437 m). Status: research pass 1 only; below T1.

## Documented (secondary sources, not drawings)

- [Wikipedia, Liglig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liglig): hilltop in Palungtar Municipality, Gorkha District, about 1,437 m. Held by a Magar king (Dalsur Ghale Magar named) before Drabya Shah of Lamjung took it, dated Vijaya Dashami 1616 BS (1559 CE). Stone ruins of an "ancient palace" are visible on the hilltop. No builder is named.
- Lead not yet read: a 2018 draft report on Ligligkot (Scribd 565537231) may hold a survey plan.

## Conflicts

1. Who was killed in 1616 BS is not named; Dalsur Ghale Magar and Mansingh Khadka Magar are both given as rulers.
2. Drabya Shah's parentage is given three ways (brother of Narahari Shah; youngest son of Yashobrahma Shah; a line via Nara Bhupal Shah).
3. The race legend (Chepeghat versus Chorkate start) is tradition, not evidence of the fort.
