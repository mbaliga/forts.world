# Red Fort, Delhi: research pass (2026-11-16, daily run)

Documented (plan): "Sketch plan of Delhi fort showing the arrangement of the buildings now demolished" (G. Sanderson 1912, after Fergusson; Commons, public domain), registered to the OSM wall (data/plans/red-fort-q45957/registration.json; plan top = east; 0.4836 m per px on the 2000 px rendering; 5 control points, residuals 8-33 m; +-30 m). Placed: Lahore and Delhi gates and barbicans, Diwan-i-Amm hall and court, Naubat Khana, Chhatta Chowk, Rang Mahal, Mumtaz Mahal, Diwan-i-Khass, Hammam, Moti Masjid, Moti Mahal, Hayat Bakhsh and Mehtab gardens, Shah, Musamman and Asad burjs.
Mapped (OSM): wall fragments (the mapped west wall has a 44 m gap at the Lahore Gate, used as the gate), buildings, roads. The trace window was widened to +-800 m.
Schematic only: walls 18 m (east/north fragments) and 33 m (west/south fragments), Wikipedia, secondary.
Missing: heights, gate passages, Salimgarh and the north end, the Nahr-i-Bahisht channel, separation of standing vs demolished buildings on the plan, pre-1857 and 1857 eras, the ASI site-plan photos (CC BY 3.0, fetched, not traced).
