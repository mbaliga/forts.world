# Rajasthan fort fidelity pass 3

This checkpoint improves five Rajasthan scenes and adds official documentation to their source records. None is certified as a faithful reconstruction yet; all five remain **improved geometry; not verified** in the audit.

## Site updates

- **Bhatner / Hanumangarh:** the enclosure now follows the parallelogram shown in the ASI site plan, the rendered perimeter carries the documented twelve bastions per side, and its catalogue point is corrected to the ASI Jodhpur Circle coordinates. Its four vertices and the bastion spacing are still normalized placeholders, not digitized from the 0–200 metre plan. The 52 bigha statement remains a source claim, not a conversion to square metres; the ward map, Ghaggar channels, town blocks, current aerial and conservation condition still need registration.
- **Ranthambore:** replaced the one-gate placeholder with seven named gate cues from the UNESCO nomination, and added schematic palace remains, the Ganesh temple, and representative tank markers. The nomination describes the north approach and angled gate sequence, but bearings and gate coordinates remain estimates until its site map is traced.
- **Gagron:** changed the circular prototype to a triangular hill-fort cue with a landward approach and small settlement context. Rajasthan Tourism describes the Ahu, Kali and Sindh river setting around three sides. The displayed water remains a simplified scene treatment, not mapped river courses.
- **Kumbhalgarh:** retained the detailed custom model and added the 2024 conservation report as an official source. That report identifies the 14 km wall circuit, named gates, later Fateh Singh palace, and important temple groups. The scene still compresses the site and estimates most internal positions.
- **Chittorgarh:** added an interactive reference point for UNESCO’s 305 ha property and 440 ha buffer, with an explicit warning that the scene perimeter has not been aligned to the official map. Existing long plateau and lower-town work remains in place.

## Evidence distinction

Official descriptions and mapped property extents are treated as documented facts. Scene sizes, local bearings, building positions, settlement density and water outlines are treated as inferred unless directly traced. Government map and tourism sources do not substitute for a measured plan or a current orthophoto registered to the game coordinate system. No scene in this pass has been promoted to terrain-ready.
