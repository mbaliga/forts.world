# Regional fort inventory: Pakistan, Afghanistan and Iran

**Status:** working inventory, source-audited through 1 November 2026. It is not a claim that every fort has been found.

## Why this is a separate evidence layer

IN-RTS needs both durable fortifications and the broader campaign geography: temporary defenses, fortified settlements, crossings, staging points and culturally significant places can shape a campaign without being forts. The map therefore keeps the fort register, campaign waypoints and heritage places in separate data layers. A recorded fort point is not a surveyed wall footprint, and an approximate monument anchor is not a surveyed coordinate.

## Afghanistan: two additions, both caveated

- **Fortress of Bost / Qala-e-Bost:** Afghanistan’s Ministry of Information and Culture reports a restoration project at the monument ([project update](https://www.moic.gov.af/en/restoration-historic-structures-bost-castle-helmand-45-complete)). Its map point is only a nearby populated-place gazetteer anchor, not a surveyed monument coordinate. The entry is marked approximate.
- **Shahr-e Zuhak / Red City:** UNESCO’s [Bamiyan Valley property](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/208/) documents the site as part of the cultural landscape. The UNESCO map longitude for Zuhak conflicts with the property’s own geographic description; the map uses an approximate geotagged monument point and records the discrepancy. It is not presented as a surveyed coordinate.

Afghanistan’s public [national places portal](https://afg.gov.af/explore/places) itself cautions that its content is not yet officially verified. It is suitable as a discovery index, not as sole authority for a verified national count. Ministry reports of other forts/sites without usable coordinates remain in the research queue rather than being assigned guessed map locations.

## Not yet mapped: candidate queue

These names surfaced during register review but were not plotted because the source point was absent, the record was ambiguous, or the record needs a stronger second source:

- **Pakistan:** Pharwala Fort and Rawat Fort (named in DOAM listing, coordinates not confirmed during this pass); Nauroze Fort (record’s name/location description conflict); Fort of Bakhtiyar Khan (district/description and point conflict); Fort of the Nawab of Tank, Gabar/Gagra/Gala Fort, Garam Chashma Fort, Doda Fort and other provincial records requiring item-by-item review.
- **Afghanistan:** Kafir Qala in Parwan and Qala Muhammad in Zaranj appear in Ministry reporting but need coordinate-grade records and independent identity review. Other national-portal entries remain discovery leads until corroborated.
- **Iran:** this pass adds only the seven components of the Alamut UNESCO property. It is not a national inventory of Iranian fortifications.

## Data and gameplay limits

1. These additions are research records; they open no playable 3D scenes.
2. Registered or approximate point coordinates should anchor regional search and campaign views, not walls, gates, roads or tactical navigation.
3. The source period is recorded as a broad source attribution. It does not imply continuous use or a precise construction date.
4. Route links and travel costs between these sites must use separate route evidence or be clearly marked as inferred gameplay modeling.
5. Continue harvesting provincial and national registers, reconcile name variants and duplicates, and add documented non-fort campaign places to their own layer.
