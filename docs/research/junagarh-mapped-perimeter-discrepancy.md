# Junagarh, Bikaner: mapped perimeter and conflicting measurements

The old enclosure was a rectangle derived from published aggregate area and circumference, with illustrative palace and city blocks. The present research scene instead uses [OpenStreetMap way 126998139](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/126998139), tagged as the fort and a city wall. Its projected vertices total approximately 1,596 m in perimeter. This is substantially greater than the roughly 986–990 m reported by government references and previously used for the scene. These measurements may describe different boundaries or contain mapping or source errors; the reason is not established. The mapped line is therefore displayed as a research trace, not a verified wall survey. © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL.

The mapped [palace-area polygon](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/803781239) is an area cue only; its individual monuments, architecture and interiors have not been modeled. A mapped [eastern driveway](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/803781219) shares a node with the mapped boundary, providing a provisional opening; its identification as Suraj Pol, dimensions and architectural form are not verified. The present mapped [Sur Sagar water outline](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/195630461) provides city context, but its water level, historical extent and the surrounding settlement remain unmodeled.

[Survey of India](https://surveyofindia.gov.in/files/bikaner.pdf) and the [2011 district census handbook](https://censusindia.gov.in/nada/index.php/catalog/1022/download/3102/DH_2011_0803_PART_A_DCHB_BIKANER.pdf) describe the fort and moat. The previous schematic moat was not mapped and has been removed until its actual trace and cross section can be established. Modern geometry does not establish the 1594, 1780 or 1800 plans; those previous scenes have been withdrawn. Obtain a measured fort/moat plan, gate and palace drawings, current orthophoto and dated historic evidence to resolve the discrepancy. Flat ground and rendered wall heights are placeholders; navigation checks do not prove fidelity.
