# Vijaydurg: mapped walls and coastal baseline

The former scene used an invented headland, buildings and two generic wall rings. The current present-day research scene uses the [UNESCO component coordinate](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1739/maps/), 16°33′39″N, 73°20′00″E, as an origin. This point and the 9.27 ha inscribed property are not the wall circuit.

Ten OpenStreetMap wall segments are included: [113617762](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/113617762), [113617789](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/113617789), [113617791](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/113617791), [147730233](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/147730233), [658582732](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/658582732), and ways 1026823414–1026823416 and 1026823418–1026823419. © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL. One segment is also mapped as coastline. The traces have gaps and may overlap: they are not an architectural survey, complete defensive circuits or a verified mapping of UNESCO’s three fortification lines. Wall height and thickness are illustrative. No gateway, building or date is assigned to them.

A part of the western shoreline follows the mapped fort-coast segment and [coastline way 113617776](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/113617776); the water polygon is artificially closed away from the shore. The mapped [modern road](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/113617759) is shown schematically. The eastern shore, Waghotan estuary, harbour and settlement remain incomplete. Terrain uses [SRTM30](https://www.opentopodata.org/datasets/srtm/) sampled at 100 m intervals on 1 November 2026; this cannot resolve walls, terraces, shore, harbour levels or bathymetry.

[UNESCO](https://whc.unesco.org/en/tentativelists/6703/) describes the triple fortification and maritime setting, but no measured and registered plan has been integrated. The prior 1756 blockout is removed because current mapping does not establish that historical arrangement. Obtain the official fort survey, detailed terrain, current orthophoto, harbour and village plans, and period-specific evidence before calling this model faithful.
