# Namakkal Fort: research pass

Alpha-U batch, 2026-11-14. Site id `namakkal-fort-q15260872`. Tier reached **T1** (status `needs-plan`). Curation: `tools/trace/curation/namakkal-fort-q15260872.mjs`; trace: `src/levels/namakkal-fort-q15260872.trace.js`. No plan, survey drawing or dated image was found, so nothing here is traced from a plan and no constructed layer is drawn. Standing rules: no invented heights, no invented gates, present-day roads and buildings on the "today" view.

## Mapped (OpenStreetMap and terrain)
- OSM way 402592136 ("Namakkal Rockfort", historic=fort, Wikidata Q15260872) outlines the rock: 162 x 81 m, 8,255 m2 (2.0 acres). Wikipedia gives about 1.5 acres on top (uncited); the mapped outline follows the rock, not the summit.
- The ledger point is 1.2 km away in the town; the build moved the origin to the outline centroid. A bare-rock zone (way 339333406, 268 x 522 m), a pond, the Mariamman Temple, another Hindu place of worship (deity not tagged), a PWD office and the town roads are mapped, ten buildings in all.
- The terrain grid reads 241 m at the outline centroid: about 165 m for the town plus the written 75 m rock.

## Documented in text (Wikipedia, not placed unless stated)
- Wikipedia: built in the 17th century in the reign of Thirumalai Nayak (cited to the Tamil Nadu government portal); walls of well-cut blocks of the hill's own stone; a Narasimha temple and a mosque inside; the Kamalalayam tank at the foot; the rock is "75 m (246 ft) tall" (uncited).
- Wikipedia: British capture in 1768 (uncited); held by Hyder Ali and Tipu Sultan; passed to the East India Company under the Treaty of Srirangapatnam (undated).

## Inferred
- The 17th-century era is the rock outline and bare-rock area carried back; no wall, gate or temple is drawn.

## Conflicts and doubts
- Two builders: Thirumalai Nayak of Madurai (cited) and Ramachandra Nayakar, a poligar of Sendamangalam (uncited).
- The page names the Department of Archaeology, the Tamil Nadu Archaeological Department and the ASI as the maintaining authority. The Peninsular War garrison claim is uncited and implausible for this fort; it is not used. Ranganatha cave temples are not in the page.

## Missing evidence for T2
- A plan from the Tamil Nadu archaeology department or the ASI: the wall line on the rock, the gate, the Narasimha temple and mosque positions, and a finer terrain model for the 75 m rock (the DEM smooths it to a broad rise).
