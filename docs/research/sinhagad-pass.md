## Documented (secondary source: en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sinhagad, read 2026-11-15; its own citations noted)
- Elevation 1,317 m AMSL, about 760 m above surrounding ground (cites Verma 1985 and Survey of India 1:50k sheet 47F/15). Other web pages give ~1,312 m: **conflict**, use DEM for terrain and treat both as unverified until the SoI sheet is read.
- Setting: about 28 km SW of Pune, on a steep cliff of the Bhuleswar range, Sahyadri.
- Two gates named: Pune Darwaja (NE) and Kalyan Darwaja (SE) (cites a Maharashtra Tourism page). Gate positions and forms: no plan source yet.
- Structures listed with no position or dimensions: stables, brewery, Kali temple, Kondhaneshwar temple, Tilak's bungalow, Rajaram cenotaph, Tanaji memorial, 1973 TV relay tower.
- Ownership timeline (the dated eras available for T3): Koli chief Nag Naik (to 14th c.) → Delhi Sultanate 1328 (after an 8-month siege) → Bahmani → Ahmadnagar → Bijapur → Shivaji 1647 / returned 1649 / retaken 1656 → Mughals 1665 (Treaty of Purandar) → Marathas 1670 → Mughals c.1689 → Marathas 1693 → Rajaram dies at the fort 3 Mar 1700 → Aurangzeb 1703 → Marathas 1706 → British 1817 → India 1947.

## Conflicts and weak points
- Retaking by Tanaji Malusare: the article says March 1670 (self-published Marathi source); popular accounts give the night of 4 Feb 1670. Not resolved; do not pin an era to either until a primary or scholarly source is read.
- Construction date: not documented. The Kondhana/Kaundinya naming and the 1647 episode are uncited in the article.
- The Tanaji memorial is "believed" ~350 years old only.
- Wikipedia lists no bastions, tanks or tombs. Absence there is not evidence of absence.
