# Amer: Maota and Panna Meena present-day geometry

The former Maota ellipse was an arbitrary shoreline. The present scene now uses the outer way 163418159 of [OpenStreetMap Maota Lake relation 15961875](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/15961875) and the mapped [Kesar Kyari island](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/192118827). The island ground is raised over a single lake water surface; water rendering and navigation do not model a separately cut-out polygon or realistic lake depth. Shoreline, water elevation and island surface are not surveyed, and the garden itself is not reconstructed. © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL.

The former Panna Meena ka Kund placeholder was at local (25,120), roughly 330 m from its mapped footprint. The present scene now uses [OSM way 410063780](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/410063780), projected into the same 3 m/unit coordinate frame as the fort. Its five-vertex outline is mapped, not a measured architectural plan. Eight stair tiers and four octagonal kiosk cues follow the [Rajasthan Tourism heritage-walk description](https://www.tourism.rajasthan.gov.in/content/dam/rajasthan-tourism/english/pdf/Heritage_Walk_Mail.pdf), but stair profile, depth, kiosk placement, materials and height remain illustrative. The mapped footprint is present-day data and does not establish a construction phase.

The mapped Maota and Kesar Kyari outlines now appear only in the present-day scene; their historical shorelines and the timing of the island garden are not established. This is not evidence the lake was absent in earlier periods. The palace, terrain, water network, Sagar lakes, town, shared ridge walls and the connection to Jaigarh still need detailed geographic registration and dated survey evidence. The [UNESCO/ICOMOS evaluation](https://whc.unesco.org/document/123033) describes the shared Amber–Jaigarh fortifications, Maota and settlement as one landscape; the separate local scenes do not yet reconstruct that connected geography.


## Jaleb Chowk and Old Amer

The previous rectangular Jaleb Chowk marker was placed schematically. The present-day scene now uses the mapped [Jaleb Chowk square](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/521281126) and a partial selection of 40 present-day Old Amer house footprints from OpenStreetMap, including [way 331513608](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/331513608). © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL. Some are simple blocks and those near the provisional approach are outline markers, all on schematic terrain: heights, entrances, roofs, construction periods, interiors and remaining structures have not been surveyed. The mapped square is a current place polygon, not a full survey of its paving, walls or gateways. The historic periods do not use these present-day footprints.

This is not a complete settlement reconstruction or a corrected palace plan. The random house cluster was removed; the actual terrain, streets, cadastral fabric and the transition between the palace and living town still need registration and architectural evidence.
