## Mapped (OpenStreetMap and terrain)
- OSM way 902551410 ("Talagad Fort", historic=fort, Wikidata Q19807538) is a strip 247 x 48 m (min rectangle), 7,248 m2, about 29 m wide on average, 14 vertices; 42 m from the ledger point.
- Two tracks (ways 22841961, 1150729964) are the only other mapped features. The first fetch failed (osm:false) and was retried on 2026-11-14.
- The terrain grid reads 259 m at the outline centroid; Wikipedia gives about 304 m (1,000 ft), so the smoothed grid sits about 45 m low on the spur.

## Documented in text (Wikipedia, not placed)
- About 18 km south of Roha; the builder is unknown; Ahmadnagar then Bijapur; Shivaji took it in 1648 (cited to a trekking website); Siddis surrounded it in 1659 and regained it later; Peshwa Bajirao I retook it in 1735; the British took it in 1818 (these last uncited).
- A two-layer wall "in good condition" around the narrow strip; rock-cut cisterns on Balekilla; four tanks on top; a Veer Maruti idol near the main gate. All uncited, none placed.

## Inferred
- The 1648 era is the mapped outline carried back, marked inferred.

## Conflicts and doubts
- Wikipedia says the strip is about 20 m wide (uncited); the mapped average is 29 m, so the outline is the same narrow form but is not confirmed to the metre.
- The claim that Shivaji's coronation clothes came from here is tagged citation needed and is not used.

## Missing evidence for T2
- A plan with the main gate, the double wall line, Balekilla and the tanks (Raigad Gazetteer or a trekking survey with coordinates), and a finer terrain model for the scarps.
