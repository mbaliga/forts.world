# Southern Karnataka and coastal forts: documentary pass 10

This pass covers Mudgal, Srirangapatna, Chitradurga, Bekal and Vellore. It replaces generic one-wall prototypes with evidence-informed layouts and better dated/site context. These are still interactive blockouts, **not verified reconstructions**. Official descriptions, one government development map, tourism photographs and written inventories were catalogued; no current aerial pixels were visually checked or registered in this pass.

## Changes by site

- **Mudgal:** Enlarged the hillock-fort landscape to approximate the district’s reported half-square-mile outer fortress and added a schematic royal-house mass. The Raichur District describes low outer walls on three sides, stone inner defenses, a Delhi Gate, and a hillock with royal houses. Neither wall circuit is traced; the outer landform is only area-constrained. [Raichur District: places of interest](https://raichur.nic.in/en/places-of-interest/), [Raichur District: Mudagal Fort](https://raichur.nic.in/en/tourist-place/mudagal-fort/)
- **Srirangapatna:** Changed the generic river-island scene to a triangular western-fort blockout with the six documented gate cues, two playable wall lines, a temple mass, six magazines and dungeon footprints. The official Karnataka development plan documents a 5 km × 1.5 km island, three fort walls, six gates and the monuments; UNESCO’s tentative-list record describes the fort in the western island area. The third wall line remains unmodeled because the generated closed circuits disconnected movement in the current scene. The actual island, river branches, wall traces and gate coordinates remain unregistered. [Karnataka Government destination plan](https://karnatakatourism.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/08/Destination-Development-Srirangapatna-Town-Mandya.pdf), [UNESCO tentative-list record](https://whc.unesco.org/en/tentativelists/5895)
- **Chitradurga:** Replaced the two-ring hill-fort abstraction with seven labeled defensive bands, four outer-gate cues, temple markers and three water-reservoir cues. The ASI description reproduced in the government census handbook distinguishes three outer lines at the hill foot and four more on the hill, with temples and rainwater storage. The generated rings do not follow the granite ridges; outer moats, the seven actual hills, 19 gateways, posterns and the interconnected water system remain to be surveyed. [ASI description in the District Census Handbook](https://www.amrut.mohua.gov.in/uploads/basic_deatils_document/2912_PART_B_DCHB_CHITRADURGA.pdf), [Karnataka Tourism: Chitradurga Fort](https://old.karnatakatourism.org/tour-item/chitradurga-fort)
- **Bekal:** Replaced the generic coastal ellipse with an approximately 40-acre keyhole silhouette and added labeled tower, stepped-tank and south-tunnel cues. Kerala Tourism describes the headland and laterite fort, observation tower, water tank, south-opening tunnel and sea-facing setting. The outline’s direction, shoreline, bastions and feature locations remain inferred. [Kerala Tourism: Bekal Fort features](https://www.keralatourism.org/bekal/bekal-fort-features.php), [Kerala Tourism: Bekal Fort](https://www.keralatourism.org/destination/bekal-fort/303/)
- **Vellore:** Replaced the generic river-island scene with a square, flatland moat-fort blockout whose estimated perimeter matches the district’s reported 3 km circumference. Added a Dravidian temple mass and present-day blockouts for the named mahals, mosque and post-1846 church. The district source names the structures and dates the fort broadly to the sixteenth century. The moat width, exact wall vertices, gate position, interior plans and phase dates remain unverified. [Vellore District: Vellore Fort](https://vellore.nic.in/tourist-place/vellore-fort/), [Vellore District: Jalakandeswarar Temple](https://vellore.nic.in/tourist-place/jalakandeshwarar-temple/)

## Verification state

Both playable periods pass scene and movement-graph checks for all five sites. This confirms functional navigation, not historical accuracy. Only Srirangapatna has a government fort-context map catalogued in this pass, and it has not been transformed into scene coordinates. No current aerial was visually compared. Next, register official site plans or measured drawings and current orthophotos; digitize outlines, ridges, moats, entrances and building footprints; then validate every dated scene against the site’s construction and ownership phases.
