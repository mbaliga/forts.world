# Udayagiri Fort (Nellore, Andhra Pradesh) - research pass 1

Site id `udayagiri-fort-q18170624`, batch 164, 14.865193 N 79.27498 E. Status: research pass 1 only; below T1.

## Documented (secondary sources, not drawings)

- [Wikipedia, Udayagiri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Udayagiri,_Andhra_Pradesh): built by Langula Gajapati, a chieftain under the Gajapatis of Odisha; extended under Gajapati and Vijayanagara rule; Krishnadevaraya took it about 1512 after an 18-month siege. Under Abdullah Arafath from 1682, his descendants holding it to 1859. Fort elevation 230 m. Thirteen strongholds or buildings, eight on the hill and five below. Town walls once enclosed the town and a hill; those walls have "almost entirely disappeared" while much of the fortification on the western hill remains. Hilltop mosque with two Persian inscriptions; Chinna and Pedda Masjid; tombs, temples, palaces and gardens inside.
- Leads not read: [Rayalaseema forts PDF](https://forumcontent.paradoxplaza.com/public/342287/Rayalseema%20Forts.pdf), [Deccan Chronicle](https://www.deccanchronicle.com/southern-states/andhra-pradesh/tale-of-udayagiri-the-forgotten-crown-of-andhra-1892357).

## Conflicts

1. The page gives 230 m as 750 ft in one place and 757 ft in another.
2. The post-Vijayanagara sequence names a ruler garbled as "Sultan Ahamed Proxy of Abdullah Arafath"; the order of Golconda or Bijapur control is unclear, and the page names neither.
3. No Reddy-period founding is stated here, so any Reddy attribution found elsewhere is unverified.
4. The page says walls enclosed a hill "1,000 feet high" while giving the fort at 230 m; the two heights are not the same measure and neither is a surveyed figure.

## Not found

No gate, bastion, tank or wall height. The eight-hill and five-below stronghold list is not enumerated.
