# Pratapgad: research pass

Alpha-U batch, 2026-11-14. Site id `pratapgad`. Tier reached **T1** (status `needs-plan`). Curation: `tools/trace/curation/pratapgad.mjs`; trace: `src/levels/pratapgad.trace.js`. No plan, survey drawing or dated image was found, so nothing here is traced from a plan and no constructed layer is drawn. Standing rules: no invented heights, no invented gates, present-day roads and buildings on the "today" view.

## Mapped (OpenStreetMap and terrain)
- OSM way 1569578753 (barrier=wall, historic=fort, Wikidata Q3582929, World Heritage tags: ref:whc 1739-007, inscribed 2025) is the outline and wall line: 645 x 328 m at its widest, 86,752 m2 (8.7 ha), 230 vertices. The terrain grid reads 1,060 m at the outline centroid; Wikipedia gives about 1,080 m.
- Three temple footprints (Tulja Bhavani, Kedareshwar, Vetal), a government guest house, two water bodies, steps, eight paths, the Pratapgad road and State Highway 72 are mapped.

## Documented in text (Wikipedia, not placed unless stated)
- Wikipedia: Shivaji had the fort built by Moropant Trimbak Pingle and it was finished in 1656; the battle was fought on 10 November 1659; the British took it in 1818; a bronze Shivaji statue was unveiled on 30 November 1957 (cited); a guest house and park of 1960 (citation needed).
- Wikipedia: an upper and a lower fort and two main entrances, with the Tulja Bhavani temple from Shivaji's time (the citation behind that line is not a relevant source). No bastion or gate is named, and no wall length or height is given.

## Inferred
- The 1659 era is the mapped outline and the Bhavani temple site carried back, marked inferred; the other two temples and the guest house are left out because their dates are not established.

## Conflicts and doubts
- Distance to Mahabaleshwar is 24 km in one place and 23 km in another; the Hiroji Indulkar architect line is [citation needed].
- Older notes name a Mahadarwaza, a Rajpuri bastion, a Hanuman shrine and a Ballekilla; none is in the page read here, so none is drawn. The old scene in maratha.js is kept.

## Missing evidence for T2
- Plans for the upper and lower fort: the two gates, the bastions, wall heights, the Bhavani temple, the Mahadarwaza and the citadel. The World Heritage nomination file (Maratha Military Landscapes of India, ref 1739) is the most likely source; it was not read.
