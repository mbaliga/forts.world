# Lohagarh: mapped present-day fort and moat

The previous fort was a generated clipped rectangle with invented bastion and building positions. It has been replaced by a present-day mapped research scene centred at approximately 27.2206°N, 77.4955°E. The origin is a local map anchor, not a surveyed point. The fort remains embedded in a living city, but its surrounding urban fabric is not yet reconstructed.

The [Sujan Ganga water relation](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/2454408) uses a mapped outer water boundary and the [fort-area polygon](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/184283578) as its inner boundary. The scene uses those outlines to dig a schematic moat and raise the fort interior. The water mesh is a single cap across the outer polygon, hidden by the raised land within; the water-level lookup still considers the inner polygon part of that cap, so this is not a complete multipolygon or hydrological simulation. Depth, water level, seasonal conditions and historic extent are unknown. © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL.

Separate mapped wall ways [567198202](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/567198202) and [567199758](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/567199758) are displayed as open paths. The two mapped bridge centre lines, [279818280](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/279818280) and [279818285](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/279818285), provide approximate crossing alignments; deck width, profile and height are illustrative. The southern gate is an outline only. Museum and Kishori Mahal polygons are displayed as generic masses on mapped footprints, not reconstructions of façades or interiors.

The [ASI inventory](https://ignca.gov.in/Asi_data/88329.pdf) and [Rajasthan urban report](https://www.lsg.urban.rajasthan.gov.in/content/dam/raj/udh/organizations/ruidp/SafeguardPolicies/IEE-Phase-IV/IEE_Bharatpur_WW_Post%20interdep%2816%20March%202023%29.pdf) describe the fort, gates and moat, but neither directly registers these OSM outlines as measured current geometry. The prior 1805 scene has been withdrawn because present mapping does not establish siege-period wall, moat or urban conditions. Measured plans, detailed topography, water surveys, gate and palace drawings, surrounding settlement, current photographs and dated historic evidence remain necessary. Navigation tests do not certify historical fidelity.
