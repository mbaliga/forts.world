# Mandu: water-site position correction

The 2025 ASI/National Monuments Authority draft provides site coordinates for Kapur Talao and several baodis. The existing scene placed two separate placeholder tanks under variants of the Kapur name and positioned four baodi placeholders independently of their published coordinates. The present-day scene now centres one Kapur cue and the Champa, Ujali, Andheri and Ancient Hindu baodi cues on their respective published site points. The duplicate Kapur placeholder was removed.

These are still schematic elliptical tank forms: a site coordinate does not establish the actual shoreline, well plan, water level, condition, orientation or the point's relation to each structure. The remaining Munj, Suraja, Rewa and Sagar cues do not have registered outlines or confirmed local positions. All these placeholder geometries are now restricted to the present scene, because the available point data does not establish their appearance or extent in any earlier campaign period. This does not imply the water features were absent historically.

ASI places Jahaz Mahal between the Munj and Kapur tanks; the current geometry does not yet reproduce their actual shorelines. See the [ASI/NMA draft with coordinate inventory and survey sheets](https://www.nma.gov.in/showfile.php?lang=2&level=1&lid=1909&ls_id=1551) and [ASI monument description](https://ignca.gov.in/Asi_data/37526.pdf). Measured plans and current aerial control are needed to reconstruct these features and date their historical configurations. The broad OSM polygon labelled Jahaj Mahal is tagged as a palace compound; it was not used as a building footprint.
