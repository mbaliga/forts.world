# Islam Garh Fort (Cholistan, Pakistan) - research pass 1

Site id `islam-garh-fort-q97014618`, batch 737, 27.855 N. Status: research pass 1 only; no geometry, plan or photo yet, so below T1.

## Documented (secondary sources, not drawings)

- Dawn, 28 March 2002 ([dawn.com/news/406061](https://www.dawn.com/news/406061)): about 100 km north of Rahim Yar Khan in Cholistan; built 1665 under Raja Rawal Siri Bheem Singh over 20 years, as "Bheem War Fort". Stone brought by ox cart from Jaisalmer state, bricks made locally. Taken in 1780 by Ikhtiar Khan Sund-i-Elahi and renamed Islam Garh; Nawab Muhammad Mubarak Khan II of Bahawalpur then defeated him. The Rajar clan lived there until the 1971 war, when shelling damaged it. Described as a ruin in a "degenerative stage".
- [Tour to Punjab](https://mail.tourtopunjab.com/destination/remains-of-islamgarh-fort): a ruined desert outpost, "traces of its bastions" standing, eroded by shifting sands; believed part of a defensive chain with Derawar and Marot.

## Conflicts

1. Builder and date: Dawn gives 1665 and a named builder; Tour to Punjab gives only "centuries ago" and no builder.
2. District: Tour to Punjab says Bahawalpur district; Dawn says north of Rahim Yar Khan. Both are in Cholistan but the ledger region should be confirmed against the coordinate.
3. Dawn's mosque date (1765) precedes the 1780 conquest it is attributed to, so one of the dates is wrong.
