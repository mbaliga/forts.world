# Nahargarh: mapped ridge and coarse terrain pass

The earlier scene generated a wall by offsetting a 100 m-wide government land corridor. That corridor is not a wall-centreline survey; the offset is withdrawn. Its 15-point boundary is retained as a separate present-day reference. The generated rows of palace rooms and generic Jaipur blocks have also been removed.

The current research scene uses the present-day mapped [western wall](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/43250088), [eastern ridge wall](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/43251555), [connecting segment](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/43251554), and [Madhavendra Bhawan outline](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/2181610). © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL. The palace is a single simplified mass, not a reconstruction of the palace's courtyards or apartments. Wall height, thickness and gateway are illustrative; the mapped geometry is not a measured survey.

A 1.4 × 1.12 km sample of [SRTM30](https://www.opentopodata.org/datasets/srtm/) elevations, sampled at 140 m spacing and interpolated, replaces the imaginary hill. At this resolution terrain is only a broad approximation, and the surrounding area outside the samples blends toward a flat placeholder. Wall platforms, rock faces, paths, buildings and the Jaipur city landscape cannot be resolved from it. Ground elevations use a 400 m reference and vertical scale of four; the on-screen elevation is an approximate height above sea level, not a surveyed point elevation.

Only a present-day research scene is offered. The current mapped wall lines and palace outline do not establish their appearance in 1734 or 1868; those historical scenes remain unavailable pending dated architectural evidence. The [2022 government corridor plan](https://forestsclearance.nic.in/DownloadPdfFile.aspx?FileName=411271232121463W04DigitalMap.pdf&FilePath=..%2Fwritereaddata%2FFormA%2FDetailsOfMaps%2F) and [Rajasthan Tourism description](https://www.tourism.rajasthan.gov.in/nahargarh-fort.html) provide context but do not measure the reconstruction. A detailed topographic survey, registered current imagery, palace plans, gateway identification and dated wall phases are required before calling the scene faithful. A navigation smoke check verifies software reachability only.
