# Taragarh, Ajmer: mapped ridge and gateway point

The former ellipse matched an approximate site area but was not a surveyed perimeter. It has been removed, together with arbitrary settlement blocks and guessed reservoir locations. The current scene shows the open [OpenStreetMap wall way 1478564262](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/1478564262), © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL. It is one mapped line, not a verified full fort circuit; its displayed height and thickness are illustrative.

The [ASI Jodhpur Circle inventory](https://www.asijodhpurcircle.in/monuments) publishes 26.443597°N, 74.616686°E for the protected Gateway of Taragarh Hill, and its [monument description](https://www.asijodhpurcircle.in/monument/ajmer/gatewayoftaragarh) calls the entrance Lakshmi Pol. The scene now anchors a provisional twin-guard-room/gateway cue at that reference point. The [ASI/IGNCA plan](https://ignca.gov.in/Asi_data/88329.pdf) shows the gateway layout, but the rendered mass, exact position within the protected site point and orientation remain inferred. The mapped wall and ASI point are separate, and no connection between them is asserted.

The ridge surface uses 121 [SRTM30 samples via OpenTopodata](https://www.opentopodata.org/datasets/srtm/), retrieved 1 November 2026 on a 140 m grid over 1.4 × 1.4 km. This is coarse surface data, not a survey of the fort, rock faces, gates or buildings. Beyond its coverage the ground blends to a placeholder. The scene retains the 400 m reference with four-to-one vertical compression; elevation readouts are approximate.

The earlier 1650 and 1795 scenes have been withdrawn because the present mapped wall and gateway point do not establish their dated architecture. The 1911 historical account's reservoir names and chronology remain accessible as research information, not located geometry. Detailed plans, current imagery, gate orientation, water systems, urban settlement and dated phases remain necessary. Navigation checks verify software reachability only.
