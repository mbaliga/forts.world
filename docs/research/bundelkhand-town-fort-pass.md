# Bundelkhand and central India town-fort pass

Five scenes have been expanded with official monument names, dated snapshots and settlement context. This is a research checkpoint only; all five remain **improved geometry; not verified** in the audit. Aerial views were not orthorectified or georeferenced in this pass.

## Scene changes

- **Kalinjar:** retained the fort’s Chandela and later Mughal snapshots, added the Neelkanth Mahadev temple, several district-listed palace and mosque cues, approximate tank features, and a Jujhotia Basti settlement cue. The district’s list does not provide a measured site plan for these positions.
- **Orchha:** replaced the single shelter with a schematic Raja Mahal, Jahangir Mahal and Rani Mahal/Ram Raja Temple ensemble, town sectors and seven Betwa channel cues. The seven-channel count comes from Niwari District Government; the channel shapes and palace plans are illustrative.
- **Chanderi:** added named monuments from Ashoknagar’s official district page and made the 1528 siege a separate date snapshot. Added town sectors, but left all locations and building footprints marked schematic. An ASI annual report is catalogued for the fort and Johar Tal context.
- **Ajaigarh:** used the Panna district account to name the north gate, southeast Tharauni gate, central Ajay Palka Talav and nearby Jain temple remains. The gate bearings and lake/temple coordinates remain estimates.
- **Narwar:** reduced the overly large generated outline and added schematic palace and town/approach blocks. The Government of India tourism source does not supply a measured plan, so those forms are still prototypes.

## Research evidence and remaining work

The sources used here are official district, tourism, UNESCO tentative-list, or ASI/IGNCA records. They support historical names, broad chronology, landscape relationships and some monument counts. They do not make the model footprints accurate by themselves. The next research task is to obtain readable measured plans and register them to current aerial and terrain data, then model each major structure only in periods supported by dated records.
