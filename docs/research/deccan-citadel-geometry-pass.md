# Deccan citadel geometry pass

This pass replaces five generated generic scenes with plan-informed local geometry for Bidar, Gulbarga, Bijapur Arkilla, Raichur and Naldurg. It is a geometry blockout, not a complete faithful reconstruction. The outlined wall paths are normalized tracings made for the local scene and have not been georeferenced to control points or validated against a current orthophoto. Do not use the model coordinates as survey data.

| Gulbarga | Government district account records double fortification, a 30 ft moat, 15 towers and 26 guns. | Raichur | A fortification study reproduces the two-fort plan from G. ## Current imagery and verification limits

Current Google Maps satellite views were visually consulted for Bidar, Gulbarga, Bijapur, Raichur and Naldurg while preparing the scene extents and checking the relationship between each fort and its urban or water setting. The imagery tiles displayed 2026 Airbus/CNES-Airbus/Maxar attribution in this session, but no acquisition date was exposed. These views are therefore recorded as visually consulted, date unknown; no claim of a measured aerial comparison or structural-condition audit is made.

Every one of these five sites remains **NOT VERIFIED** in the fidelity audit. A site can move beyond this state only after source maps are digitized with scale and orientation, transformed to a coordinate reference system, checked against dated orthophotos/DEM or official aerial material, and reviewed against dated architectural records. Approximate scene coordinates remain separate from the catalogue's discovery coordinates.
