# Chittorgarh boundary and reference-point reconciliation

Reviewed 1 November 2026. This pass reconciles official location and extent claims before changing the playable fort geometry. It identifies a boundary-registration issue; it does not certify the scene perimeter.

## Reference coordinates

- ASI Jodhpur Circle lists “Fort of Chitor as a whole” at 24.887222° N, 74.646944° E. The playable level’s discovery coordinate matches this official whole-site point.
- UNESCO’s 2013 nomination gives N24°53′00″ E74°38′46″ as the Chittorgarh nomination reference coordinate. The two reference points are approximately 441 m apart. They should remain separate until the nomination map and ASI point can be tied to local survey controls; shifting the 3D model to either point would currently assert an unsupported alignment.

## Conflicting extents

The UNESCO dossier identifies the component property as 305 ha with a 440 ha buffer and includes separate topographic, satellite and property/buffer maps. Its prose gives multiple dimensions: the mesa extends 5.6 km north–south with about 0.75 km average width; the wall perimeter is about 4.5 km; and another passage describes a 5 km wall stretch and 0.91 km width. The 1971 1:50,000 topographic map and satellite image are cited in the dossier, but their boundaries have not been digitized and registered against the scene in this pass.

The current local rim follows the dossier’s 5.6 km mesa-length description, while its two long wall runs and lateral rim remain schematic. The cited perimeter and width statements do not agree sufficiently to justify scaling the model to one number. The ASI 305 ha property boundary, the natural mesa edge, the UNESCO buffer, and the defensive wall must be reconstructed as separate layers.

## Scene and audit change

The existing fort marker remains on ASI’s “Fort of Chitor as a whole” coordinate. The UNESCO reference point and dimensional conflict now appear in the boundary POI and audit. The scene stays explicitly unverified until the untrimmed 1971 map, property/buffer map, satellite sheet, and current aerial can be registered to stable controls and compared with the ASI plan sheets. No geometry was shifted from the conflicting prose measurements.
