# Agra Fort: research pass (2026-11-16, daily run)

Documented (plan): Stanford's 1901 guide-book plan "The Fort, Agra" (Commons File:Plan du fort d'Agra (1901).jpg, public domain) registered to the OSM wall by a single similarity fit (data/plans/agra-fort/registration.json, scale 1.50 m/px, rotation 3.1 degrees, +-25 m). Placed from it: Delhi Gate, Elephant Gate, Amar Singh Gate, Water Gate (snapped to the mapped wall), Moti Masjid courtyard, the Armoury Square (Diwan-i-Amm court), the Jahangiri Mahal block, and label positions for Saman Burj, Khas Mahal, Shish Mahal, Machchi Bhawan and the marble baths.
Mapped (OSM): perimeter wall with bastion bumps, internal wall lines, buildings, Colvin's grave node (also on the plan, 24 m apart).
Schematic only: 21 m wall (Britannica, secondary).
Missing: any heights on the documented layer, gate forms, a measured ASI drawing (Commons "Site Plan - Agra Fort" photo, CC BY 3.0, fetched but not traced), the Yamuna on the east side and the moat, Diwan-i-Khas, Anguri Bagh, Naginah Masjid footprints, earlier eras. Dropped: two OSM waterways on the SW ravine side, one labelled Yamuna, contradicting the plan.
Not used: CC BY-SA plans (Map of Agra Fort 2012).
