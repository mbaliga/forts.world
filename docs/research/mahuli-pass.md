# Mahuli Fort (Thane, Maharashtra) - research pass 1

Site id `mahuli-fort-q97309169`, batch 86, 19.48135 N 73.249272 E in the ledger (Wikipedia gives 19 28 46 N 73 14 18 E, about 1 km west; check against OSM once geometry lands). Status: research pass 1 only; below T1.

## Documented (secondary sources, not drawings)

- [Wikipedia, Mahuli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahuli): about 75 km north-east of Mumbai; the highest point in Thane District, 2,815 ft (about 858 m). Open Shiva temple, a small perennial cistern, three caves, and a stone arch called Kalyan Darwaja with a broken dome. Pinnacles Vazir and Vishnu nearby. History: Shahaji and the young Shivaji at Mahuli 1635-36; Shivaji took it from the Mughals 8 January 1658; lost 1661 and 1665 (Treaty of Purandar); failed attempt February 1670 under Manohardas Gaud's command; captured 16 June 1670 by Moropant Pingle; held by Maratha successors until 1817, then the British. Declared protected; restoration plan prepared 2015.
- Trek pages read as leads only: [durgbharari.in/mahuli](https://durgbharari.in/mahuli/), [fortsandtreks](https://fortsandtreks.blogspot.com/2018/03/mahuligad-fort.html). Mahuli is a three-fort group with Bhandargad and Palasgad.
