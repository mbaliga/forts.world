Ratnagiri candidate IDs include district prefixes to avoid conflating names such as Bhairavgad, Goa Fort and Yashwantgad with similarly named sites elsewhere. Exact-name deduplication only is used on import; aliases remain a review task. Taluka membership follows the district tourism table; it is not a verified coordinate or historical administrative boundary.

The previously researched temples, stepwells and settlement nodes remain intact in `content/historical-world/`. They will be reconciled into the connected-world schema rather than silently copied into the fort-only map.

The map now offers reviewable name/country matches between the 242 project records and Smith–Newton study entries. An initial run found suggestions for 138 project entries (151 candidate pairs). This is a heuristic starting point, not a count of verified matches or complete regional coverage. Name-only candidates and repeated names require particular care.
