Solid points are forts whose founding year Wikidata records, shown from that year on. Faint points are the many more whose founding year is not recorded yet; they show at every year, so India and much of Asia and Africa are not left empty. Rings are forts rebuilt in 3D. Contours are the world's relief (ETOPO1, about 18 km cells), finer as you zoom. Borders are today's (Natural Earth). Empires over time need an openly licensed historical boundaries dataset; we have not found one yet. A person's world is shaped from the places their own writings name, so its edges are approximate; routes join those places in order.
